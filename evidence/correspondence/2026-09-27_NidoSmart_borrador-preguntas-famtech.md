## Lote 1 — Expansion & layout (enviar primero; bloquea la decisión de cimentación/obra civil)

**Estado:** enviado 2026-09-28, respondido 2026-09-28 — ver [respuesta de FamTECH](2026-09-28_FamTECH_email_respuesta-lote1-expansion-layout.rtfd/) e integración en `evidence/quotations/README.md`, `docs/business-plan/07-diseno-fisico.md`, `docs/business-plan/06-crecimiento-modular.md`, `docs/business-plan/08-automatizacion.md` y `docs/investment-project/05-ficha-tecnica-nave.md`.

**Subject:** NidoSmart – Expansion & layout questions before finalizing civil works design (5-set / 640-bird order, quotation BS04H092126)

Dear FamTECH team,

Thank you again for the detailed information you've already provided on the cage system, feeding, egg collection, and manure removal (quotation v4, BS04H092126). Before we finalize the civil works design, we need your help closing the following questions on expansion and layout. We have more technical questions on ventilation, water/manure handling, and electrical specs, which we will send in separate follow-ups to keep each email manageable.

**Expansion & shared equipment (highest priority):**

Our long-term plan is to grow this installation beyond the current 5 sets (640 birds) by adding sets along the same building, using the same shared feeding, egg-collection, and manure-removal line — as you described for your reference layout of up to 90 cage groups (~11,520 birds) sharing one system.

1. We noticed that your reference layout of 90 groups in a 120 m house implies a density of about 1.33 m per cage group, while our own order (17 m for 5 sets) works out to about 3.4 m per set — roughly 2.5× more space per group. Because of this difference, we don't think the 90-group / ~11,520-bird figure is necessarily the real ceiling for OUR specific layout: at our own density, the confirmed 100 m travel path of the skip-hoist feeder would only reach about 29 sets (~3,700 birds), well below 90 groups. Could you confirm, for our specific 17 m / 5-set configuration (not the generic 120 m reference layout), how many additional sets the SAME feeding and egg-collection line we already ordered can actually support before a second complete shared system would be required?
2. Separately, the skip-hoist feeder specification states "a travel path of up to 100 meters." Does this limit apply to the specific unit in our order, or is it a general catalog figure? If our installation reaches that limit, would a second feed line be required, or can the equipment be specified now for a longer travel path?
3. Can the foundation/civil structure you recommend be extended lengthwise in stages (adding sets over time) without rebuilding the foundation? If so, after how many sets (or what length) would a structural reinforcement or expansion joint be required?
4. Please confirm the real manure-removal belt throughput (kg/h). Our target: the same belt (1.0×1130 mm, already quoted) must be able to remove approximately 1,000 kg/day without buildup once we reach our medium-term target of ~5,120 birds — well below your declared ceiling of ~11,520 birds. Can the currently quoted belt support this, or would a different model be needed at that scale?

**Layout & dimensions:**

5. What is the actual clear height required by the fully installed system (4-level cage stack + skip-hoist feeder rail + egg-collecting machine above the top level)? We need to confirm our 4.2 m total building height is sufficient.
6. What is the real longitudinal footprint of each of the 5 sets, including the front-end components (hopper/feeder gearbox) and the backend components (egg-collection drive unit, manure-belt drive unit)? We need to confirm the 5 sets fit within 17 m with circulation space at both ends.
7. What is the transverse footprint (cage depth + central aisle) of the double-sided, 4-level arrangement? We need to confirm it fits within our 4 m width.
8. Feeder trough space: how many linear cm of feeding space per bird does your set provide?

Given that quotation v4 pricing is only valid through 2026, we'd like to close these first, as they affect our civil works decision the most. Thank you again for your continued support.

Best regards,
